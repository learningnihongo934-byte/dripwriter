DripWriter

Types your text into any app, one keystroke at a time, exactly as written.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/learningnihongo934-byte/dripwriter/main/install.sh | bash

Paste into Terminal on macOS 14 or newer. Installs the app and the dripwriter command. No Mac? Try the web version.

What it does

Types anywhereDocs, Notes, Slack, Discord, VS Code, browsers, terminals.
Finish in…Pick a speed, or a deadline and let it work out the pace.
Five rhythmsHuman, Steady, Nervous, Burst and Drip.
Real typosHits a neighboring key, backspaces, fixes it.
Floating panelProgress, pause and stop over any app.
Safe stopsEsc stops. Switching apps pauses; coming back resumes.
Chat and code readyShift+Return new lines, skip auto-indent.
LibrarySnippets, history, drag-in files, lifetime stats.

Shortcuts

⌃⌥⌘VType the clipboard at your cursor
⌃⌥⌘DStart or pause
⌃⌥⌘↑ ⌃⌥⌘↓Faster or slower while typing
escStop

From the terminal

dripwriter "hello world"            # type into the front app
dripwriter -f essay.txt -w 90 -T 2  # 90 wpm, 2% typos
pbpaste | dripwriter -t 5m          # finish in five minutes
dripwriter --dry -f essay.txt       # how long would it take?
